University College Cork is an internationally renowned university where excellence in teaching and research is aligned with an inclusive and respectful environment, where all staff and students can flourish and thrive. Founded in 1845, UCC is a comprehensive research-intensive institution comprising four academic Colleges, delivering a rich tradition of collaborative teaching, research and scholarship that has true global impact.  UCC has over 26,000 students from over 130 countries and over 3,000 diverse staff from all over the world. UCC is ranked in the top 1.5% of global universities (https://www.ucc.ie/en/discover/rankings/), ranked 1st in the world for sustainability and the top Irish university for research impact and industry collaboration. UCC has a firm commitment to Equality, Diversity and Inclusion as demonstrated by a recent silver Athena Swan award. UCC is located on a beautiful, green urban campus within walking distance of the city centre of Cork. Cork is the vibrant second city of the Republic of Ireland, with one of the world's largest natural harbours and a beautiful Atlantic coastline, offering extensive recreational opportunities in close proximity to the city.

Academic Administrator, School of Computer Science and Information Technology

Permanent Whole Time Post

UCC wishes to appoint an administrator in the School of Computer Science and Information Technology responsible for the academic administration of postgraduate taught and research programmes and contribute to the academic administration of undergraduate programmes, as well as general school admin tasks that may arise from time to time, as appropriate.
Reporting to the School Manager, the appointee will be accountable for taught postgraduate programmes, including student recruitment and admission support, administration of programme changes, governance, coordination of examinations, external examiner reviews, and timetabling management.  In addition, the administrator will be responsible for the administration of the PhD lifecycle (approx. 70 students), from registration to graduation, with particular oversight of the PhD review process in conjunction with the new UCC Doctoral Institute.
The administrator is expected to drive digital process improvements using tools such as SharePoint, power automate, and Power BI.

Senior Research Coordinator (Technical and Data Management Officer) Rinn Artificial Intelligence
24 Months Fixed-Term Whole-Time Post
This role is part of a national operations team for a national research centre related to data science and AI. The Technical and Data Management Officer role is a national role and will support sites in ensuring that all Centre research is carried out in a GDPR compliant environment and best practices are used for storage and retrieval of datasets and reporting data. This role will work across all Sites and engage with the Site teams and their respective institutional supports to ensure all data storage is secure and data collected is relevant and compliant with all relevant regulations and best practices. This role reports to the Centre Manager and has as Line Manager the institutional lead of the host site of this position. The Technical and Data Management Officer is a key member of the Centre's national operations team and will support all participating Sites to ensure research data is managed securely and in accordance with GDPR, FAIR data management principles, and other relevant regulatory frameworks and funder requirements.
