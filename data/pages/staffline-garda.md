An Garda Síochána is the national policing and security service of Ireland, with approximately 14,000 Gardaí, 3,400 Garda staff and approximately 350 Garda Reserve members – a total workforce of almost 18,000. The mission of An Garda Síochána is “Keeping People Safe”.

It is a dynamic organisation in which its personnel can enjoy a rewarding and worthwhile career of service to the communities and people in Ireland.

An Garda Síochána as an employer offers a range of skilled specialist roles for Garda staff including those in Finance and Services, Occupational Health and Wellbeing, Data and Technology, Human Resources and People Development, Strategy and Transformation, Legal, Communications, Administration and Management.

An Garda Síochána is led by the Garda Commissioner, who is appointed by the Government.

More information about An Garda Síochána, its services and departments is available on www.garda.ie.
